Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026  ·  Developer: Utkarsh Mehta

Z Jump (the “Game”) is developed and published by Utkarsh Mehta (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy describes how gameplay information is stored on your device and how third-party advertising services may process information when you play the Game.

1. Information handled by the Game

Z Jump does not require you to register an account or directly submit your name, email address, or other personal details to play.

The Game stores gameplay data locally on your device, such as scores, high scores, progress, and game preferences, as applicable. We do not operate a dedicated server for storing this gameplay information.

2. Advertising and information collected by third parties

Z Jump uses Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC, to display banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information including:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system, and technical information
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and performance data
  • Diagnostic information and fraud-prevention signals

The precise information processed may depend on your device, app configuration, privacy choices, and applicable law.

3. Why information is used

Locally stored gameplay information supports game functionality and preferences. Advertising-related information may be processed to deliver and measure ads, provide rewarded ad experiences, protect against fraud, and maintain advertising services.

4. Third-party privacy policies

For more information about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be offered choices to consent, decline, or manage advertising preferences. Where required, the Game provides a way to revisit those choices. You may also manage available advertising settings on your device.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. You can generally remove it by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game, subject to any operating-system backup or restore features. We do not retain a separate server-side copy of this gameplay information.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices. You can review Google's privacy controls through the links above.

7. Children's privacy

Z Jump is a casual game. Its advertising configuration and data-handling practices are intended to follow applicable age-related requirements, including Google Play policies and relevant children's privacy laws. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We aim to limit the information handled directly by the Game. Third-party advertising providers maintain their own security practices. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. International processing

Google and its partners may process advertising-related information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated effective date.
